# Rejection and deprioritization register

Consolidated **7 October 2026**. Consult this before reopening a reviewed candidate. The [baseline table](current-baseline.md#canonical-candidate-status) alone owns current status; this register preserves reasons, dated pins and original verdict scope. Research decisions do not change test results. A source incompatibility leaves runtime NOT_RUN.

Reopening requires a supported release/configuration/API delta, new rights/entitlement, or a materially different confirmed use case addressing the recorded reason. A nicer UI, changed name, marketing claim or engine substitution is insufficient. Dates identify the supporting review/decision, not an invented first rejection date.
